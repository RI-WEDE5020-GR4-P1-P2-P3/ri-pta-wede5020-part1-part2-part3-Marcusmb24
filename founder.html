Meet The Founder

Bridging Basketball, Tech, and Sneaker Culture in South Africa

Behind The Brand

Nhletelo Marcus Mathebula

Born and raised in the small town of Giyani in Limpopo, Nhletelo Marcus Mathebula grew up with an unrelenting passion for two things: basketball and technology. Spending endless hours honing his skills on the court, Nhletelo developed a deep appreciation for sneaker culture—where performance footwear intersects with identity, athletic drive, and street fashion.

As an IT developer and active hoop player, Nhletelo recognized a distinct gap in the local retail market: acquiring authentic, high-heat basketball sneakers and iconic retros outside of major metropolitan hubs was frustratingly difficult. Combining his software development background with his firsthand knowledge of court performance, he founded KicksVault Retailers.

Today, Nhletelo continues to engineer seamless digital shopping experiences while supplying ballers and sneakerheads across South Africa with 100% authentic kicks.

Card Acquiring & Security

Card transactions will be acquired for KicksVault via approved South African acquiring banks. All online transactions utilize Secure Socket Layer (SSL3) encryption technology to protect your data. No sensitive card details are stored directly on our servers.
