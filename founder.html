Meet The Founder

Bridging Basketball, Tech, and Sneaker Culture in South Africa

Behind The Brand

Nhletelo Marcus Mathebula

Born and raised in Giyani, Limpopo, Nhletelo grew up with a passion for basketball and technology. Recognizing a gap in the local retail market for authentic high-heat sneakers, he combined his software development background with his knowledge of court performance to found KicksVault.

Payment Methods

Accepted Methods

ONLINE: Payment may be made via Visa and MasterCard, as well as Direct Bank Transfer (EFT). Please contact us should you wish to pay via EFT.
INSTORE: All major bank cards, SnapScan, and cash payments.

Card Acquiring & Security

Card transactions will be acquired for KicksVault via approved South African acquiring banks. All online transactions utilize Secure Socket Layer (SSL3) encryption technology to protect your data. No sensitive card details are stored directly on our servers.
