1. How do you guarantee that your sneakers are 100% authentic?

We take authenticity extremely seriously. Every single pair of sneakers that arrives at our KicksVault warehouse undergoes a rigorous, multi-point inspection process before it is approved for sale.

Our authentication team consists of industry veterans who inspect the following details:
• Stitching & Craftsmanship: We check the precision of the stitching, the quality of the threading, and the overall build quality against official manufacturer standards.
• Materials: We analyze the leather, suede, mesh, and rubber compounds to ensure they match the exact specifications of the original release.
• Tags & Labels: We verify the inner size tags, font styles, and manufacturing dates.
• Scent & Packaging: Believe it or not, authentic factory glue has a distinct smell, and the box, tissue paper, and extra laces must all match the original retail packaging.

If a pair fails even one of these checks, it is rejected. We offer a 100% money-back guarantee if any pair is ever proven to be inauthentic.

4. How do I know what size to order?

Getting the right size is crucial! Nike and Jordan sneakers generally fit true to size (TTS) for most people. However, different models can fit slightly differently.

• Air Force 1s: These tend to run slightly large and wide. If you have narrow feet, you may want to go half a size down.
• Air Jordan 1s: These are generally true to size, but can feel slightly narrow at first. They will break in after a few wears.
• Basketball Performance Shoes: If you plan to play on the court and wear thick basketball socks, you may want to go half a size up.

We highly recommend using our Size Guide (available on the Home page) to convert your UK size to US and EU sizes. If you are still unsure, please use our Contact page to reach out to us with any specific questions about a shoe's fit before you buy.

5. How do I care for and clean my premium sneakers?

To keep your KicksVault sneakers looking brand new for years, follow these essential care tips:

• Protect them first: We recommend spraying new sneakers with a stain and water repellent spray (like Crep Protect) before you wear them for the first time.
• Cleaning Leather: Use a soft-bristle brush and a dedicated sneaker cleaning solution. Avoid soaking the leather in water. Wipe away dirt gently and use a microfiber cloth to dry.
• Cleaning Suede: Never use water on suede! Use a specialized suede brush and eraser to lift dirt.
• Laces and Insoles: Remove the laces and insoles and wash them separately with warm water and mild soap.
• Drying: Never put your sneakers in the washing machine or tumble dryer. Allow them to air dry at room temperature, away from direct sunlight or heaters, which can warp the shape or cause the glue to melt.
• Storage: Store your sneakers in a cool, dry place. Keep them in their original box or a shoe bag to prevent dust and yellowing of the midsole.
